## Treatments

- **Open expression** — the lightest continuation of A2. Open smiling eyes and a small double smile borrow character from the approved art. Closed eyes and a flat mouth signal pause; upright eyes and a small open mouth request attention without looking angry.
- **Quiet visor** — the same expressions inside two fine faceted brackets. The silhouette gives stronger robot character, with slightly more visual density at 32 px. The functional glyphs share the first treatment's structure with square terminals and one small source-grid corner cut.

Recommendation: **Open expression**. It leaves the text in charge, remains airy in the 96 × 48 display slot, and keeps the larger robot silhouette available as the restrained alternative. At 32 px the mouth is intentionally simplified; the adjacent state label remains mandatory. The reconnect arrow is familiar but generic, so it always carries “Reconnect access.” Neither treatment is intended as a launcher mark.
