Recommendation: Open expression

It keeps the lightness of A2 and gives the small face a recognizable double smile. Quiet visor adds robot character, but its extra contour is more noticeable at 32 px. The functional glyphs deliberately stay close: counts for activity, an app grid for sources, familiar pause/play and reconnect.

Both remain exploratory. The authored app mark is unchanged. No production or canonical design changes.
