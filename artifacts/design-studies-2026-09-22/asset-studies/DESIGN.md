# Asset-study direction

Scope: two provisional treatments of expression and control assets inside the accepted Pocket console A2. Surface mode: Operate. The user checks whether local code extraction is ready, pauses it, chooses sources, or restores notification access. The established light cream shell and charcoal display remain authoritative.

Preserve paper `#f5f0e5`, ink `#292d2b`, casing `#e9e1d1`, apricot `#efad7c`, mint `#c2e5c8`, muted `#625e53`, line `#bcb3a0`. Reuse A2's Bricolage, Atkinson and Fragment Mono fonts. All layout dimensions and text are inherited; utility icons increase from 18 to the requested 20 px within the existing 48 px targets.

Open expression uses unframed emoticon geometry, rounded terminals, smiling angular eyes and a small double smile. Quiet visor adds two open, faceted 1.5 px display brackets with `#8d9c91` strokes; its control glyphs use square terminals and bevel joins. The two options share state geometry and semantic glyph mapping to keep the comparison focused.

Compact expressions have a separately tuned 32-unit drawing, while display expressions occupy A2's 96 × 48 slot. Ready, pause and access-off differ by geometry and visible neighboring text. Color reinforces state. No motion is required. Activity is aggregate bars, Sources is the selected-app grid, and the remaining controls retain familiar pause/play/reconnect shapes.

No new logo, launcher, platform claim or production design decision is established by these studies. Selection remains with the user and parent design task.
